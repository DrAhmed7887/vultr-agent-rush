## 1. Number and place value

Year 6: read, write, order and compare numbers up to 10,000,000; round to any degree of accuracy; use negative numbers in context and calculate intervals across 0.

- Round 3,476,512 to the nearest 10,000 → **3,480,000**
- What is the value of the 7 in 4,705,213? → **700,000**
- ★ **Intervals across 0.** The temperature goes from −4 °C to 7 °C. How many degrees did it rise? → **11**
  - Ahmes's wrong rule: *"Ignore the minus signs and take away"*, so 7 − 4 = **3**.
  - −8 to 15 → **23** (Ahmes says 7). −12 to −3 → **9**. Here Ahmes is right by luck, because the numbers don't cross 0.
  - A child's rule: *"Count up from the negative number to 0, then add the rest."*

## 2. The four operations

Year 6: long multiplication and long division; common factors, multiples and primes; **order of operations**; estimation.

- 2,347 × 36 = **84,492**
- 4,532 ÷ 18 = **251 remainder 14** = 251 7⁄9 ≈ 251.78
- Common factors of 24 and 36: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, **12**. Primes between 30 and 50: 31, 37, 41, 43, 47.
- ★ **Order of operations.**
  - Ahmes's wrong rule: *"Work from left to right."*

  | Sum | Right answer | Ahmes says |
  |---|---|---|
  | 2 + 3 × 4 | **14** | 20 |
  | 20 − 6 ÷ 2 | **17** | 7 |
  | 18 − 3 × 4 + 2 | **8** | 62 |
  | 5 + 12 ÷ 4 × 2 | **11** | 8½ |
  | (2 + 3) × 4 | **20** | 20 (right by luck) |

  - A child's rule: *"Do brackets first, then times and divide, then add and take away."*

## 3. Fractions, decimals and percentages

Year 6:
- simplify;
- compare and order fractions, including fractions bigger than 1;
- add and subtract with different denominators and mixed numbers;
- multiply proper fractions;
- divide a fraction by a whole number;
- multiply and divide by 10, 100 and 1,000;
- fraction, decimal and percentage equivalents.

- Simplify 18⁄24 → **3⁄4**
- 1 3⁄4 + 2 2⁄3 → **4 5⁄12** (53⁄12). *Ahmes's current fraction skill, with mixed numbers.*
- ★ **Compare fractions.**
  - Ahmes's wrong rule: *"The bigger bottom wins"*.
  - It's the same kind of mistake as "longer decimals are bigger".

  | Pair | Bigger | Ahmes says |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1⁄4 or 1⁄8 | **1⁄4** | 1⁄8 |
  | 2⁄9 or 2⁄7 | **2⁄7** | 2⁄9 |
  | 3⁄5 or 2⁄3 | **2⁄3** | 3⁄5 |
  | 5⁄6 or 7⁄8 | **7⁄8** | 7⁄8 (right by luck) |

  - A child's rule: *"Make the bottoms the same, then the bigger top wins."*
- Multiply fractions.
  - 1⁄2 × 1⁄3 = **1⁄6**; 2⁄3 × 3⁄4 = **1⁄2**; 3⁄5 × 5⁄6 = **1⁄2**.
  - The documented wrong idea is *"multiplying always makes it bigger"*: children expect 1⁄2 × 1⁄3 to be more than 1⁄2.
- Divide a fraction by a whole number: 1⁄3 ÷ 2 = **1⁄6**; 3⁄4 ÷ 3 = **1⁄4**; 4⁄7 ÷ 2 = **2⁄7**.
- ★ **Multiply by 10, 100 and 1,000.**
  - Ahmes's wrong rule: *"Add a zero"*.
  - It works for whole numbers and fails for decimals.

  | Sum | Right answer | Ahmes says |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0.45 × 10 | **4.5** | 0.450 |
  | 3.7 × 100 | **370** | 3.700 |
  | 0.206 × 1,000 | **206** | 0.206000 |
  | 4.8 × 1,000 | **4,800** | 4.8000 |

  - Dividing: 45 ÷ 100 = **0.45**; 7 ÷ 1,000 = **0.007**.
  - A child's rule: *"Every digit moves one place to the left for each zero."*
  - This reuses Ahmes's decimal format and number line.
- Fraction, decimal and percentage equivalents:

  | Fraction | Decimal | Percentage |
  |---|---|---|
  | 3⁄8 | 0.375 | 37.5% |
  | 1⁄5 | 0.2 | 20% |
  | 7⁄20 | 0.35 | 35% |
  | 2⁄3 | 0.666… | 66.7% (to 1 dp) |

## 4. Ratio and proportion

- 25% of 80 = **20**; 15% of 60 = **9**; 35% of 200 = **70**; 12.5% of 64 = **8**.
- Share 35 sweets in the ratio 2 : 3 → **14 and 21**. Share 63 in the ratio 2 : 5 → **18 and 45**.
- A recipe for 4 needs 300 g of flour. For 6 people → **450 g**.

## 5. Algebra

- Sequence 3n + 2: **5, 8, 11, 14, 17**. The 20th term is **62**.
- a + b = 10 and a − b = 4 → **a = 7, b = 3**.
- Pairs with x + y = 6, using whole numbers from 0: (0,6), (1,5), (2,4), (3,3), (4,2), (5,1), (6,0). That's **7 pairs**.

## 6. Measurement

- 5 miles ≈ **8 km**. 2.375 kg = **2,375 g**.
- Area of a triangle with base 9 cm and height 6 cm = **27 cm²**. Parallelogram 8 cm × 5 cm = **40 cm²**.
- Volume of a cuboid 4 cm × 3 cm × 2.5 cm = **30 cm³**.
- A 4 × 4 square and a 2 × 8 rectangle both have area 16, but their perimeters are **16** and **20**.

## 7. Geometry

- Triangle angles 47° and 68° → the third is **65°**. On a straight line, 125° → **55°**. Around a point, 90° and 110° → **160°**.
- A circle with radius 7 cm has diameter **14 cm**.
- Reflect the point (3, −2) in the x-axis → **(3, 2)**.

## 8. Statistics

- The mean of 4, 7, 9 and 12 → **8**. The mean of 15, 15, 18, 22 and 30 → **20**.
- On a pie chart of 60 children, 15 chose football. The angle is **90°**.
