The problem

Children hold on to classic maths bugs.
Explaining the rule is how they let go.

Misconceptions documented in the research

“0.45 is bigger than 0.5, because it has more digits!”

Steinle & Stacey: about 40% of Year 5 children

“1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5”

Stafylidou & Vosniadou; Lortie-Forgues, Tian & Siegler

Learning by teaching

  • Teaching a (virtual) classmate helps the teacher learn: the protégé effect (Chase et al. 2009).
  • Betty’s Brain and SimStudent are teachable agents.
  • AlgoBo / TeachYou (CHI 2024): an LLM tutee that writes code from what adults teach it.
  • BUGGY (1978): computers diagnosed children’s arithmetic bugs. Ahmes turns that around.
Ahmes is named after the scribe who copied the Rhind Papyrus around 1550 BC.Learning outcomes for children are untested.
How it works

Your words, then the literal compiler, the guard, the sandbox, exact checking.

1

Your words

The child explains the rule in plain language.

“Look at the tenths first.”

2

Literal compiler

An LLM on Vultr Serverless Inference may only implement what was said. Every step quotes the child.

qwen3.8-27b · temp 0

3

The guard

An allowlist over the code’s syntax tree. Anything unexplained or unsafe is refused before a container starts.

first layer only

4

The sandbox

A brand-new container on a Vultr VM: no network, no secrets, 64 MB, 3 seconds. Then destroyed.

removal verified

5

Exact checking

Integer arithmetic grades 40 problems. Plain maths, never AI. A read-out says which rule he now behaves like.

no floats, no LLM

Vague words give partly wrong code, and that is the lesson: the compiler cannot quietly fix the maths.

Measured: 12 of 13 test sentences compiled as expected (spike gate G1, 4 Nov).Teach Ahmes

Beyond the guard

Raw Python straight into the same container flags.

Connect to 1.1.1.1:80Network is unreachable (Errno 101)
Write to /runner.pyPermissionError: read-only disk
Look for API keysNone in the environment
Fork bombBox stopped after about 5 processes (gVisor, limit 64)

Four kill layers: timeout inside the container (3 s) · host timer docker kill (3.5 s) · a sweeper for any box older than 10 s · stripped builtins.

Every run writes a downloadable JSON receipt: container ID, limits, time, exit code, OOM flag, removal verified.

Every box runs under gVisor (runsc), its own user-space kernel. Honest limit: not a microVM.Teach Ahmes

Honest limits

  • Prototype. Learning outcomes for children are untested.
  • gVisor, not a microVM. The guard is a first layer; the gVisor container is the boundary.
  • Inference runs in the US: typed text and synthetic voices only.
