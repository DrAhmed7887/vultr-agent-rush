# Review: Teach Ahmes opening (draft 1, 4 Nov 2026)

Output: `video/out/opening.mp4`. 26.3 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 4.6 MB.

## Creative calls
- **One storyline.** It was agreed in chat (the bug, then teaching, then the dizzy rule), so no alternative storylines were drawn up.
- **Ahmes is the in-app SVG**, so he matches the real app when the film cuts to the screen recording.
- **The child is never drawn.** Her words are a speech bubble that points to the viewer. Her voice is a stock ElevenLabs voice, not a real child.
- **"Words become code" happens in one slot.** The bubble flies into his antenna, and the code types in where the bubble was.
- **The sandbox is a faience box.** It drops over him, its timer runs to 3.0 s, it stamps "Stopped", and it lifts away. The fractions go back to 1/2 + 1/3, showing that he keeps his old brain.

## Measured
- `check`: passes.
- The mix: −16.1 LUFS integrated, −2.8 dBTP true peak.
- The sound was measured, not heard. Listen for the level of the child's voice against the music.

## Kept warnings
- `svg_round_cap_hidden_dash`: the rings stay at opacity 0 until they draw, so no stray dot shows.
- `rotation_pivot_drift`: the spin is about his body centre on purpose, and the arm turns about its shoulder.
