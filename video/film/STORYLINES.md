# Teach Ahmes: whole-film storylines (HyperFrames + ElevenLabs)

The one thing: a child's words become real code, and a sandbox on Vultr makes sure bad words can't hurt anything.
The fixed rule: the containment moment must be **real**. Every claim about the sandbox is shown with the live recording or a real receipt, placed inside the animated world. It is never drawn.

## A. Ahmes's school day (recommended)
**Why:** one world from start to finish, so there are no seams, and the proof sits inside the story.

- **Device and form:** a character does the work, one frame that rebuilds.
- **What you see:** the graph-paper world from the opening runs the whole film. Every time a claim needs proof, the real app or a real receipt slides in as an object in that world: Ahmes's tablet, the sandbox's tab, a report card.
- **First 2 s:** Ahmes proudly circles 0.45. A red ✗ stamps it.
- **Beats:**
  1. The bug.
  2. The child teaches, and the tablet shows the real app scoring 24/40.
  3. The full rule, and the tablet shows the real 40/40.
  4. The dizzy rule: the box, the spin, then the box flips round to show the real receipt.
  5. Break-him tests: out of memory and the network, each a real receipt card.
  6. Thoth.
  7. Six lessons on a map.
  8. Architecture as the machine under the desk.
  9. The report card.
  10. End card.
- **Look:** the app's own graph paper, Nunito, lapis, gold and faience. Real footage sits in a rounded device frame.
- **Sound:** the narrator, the child, and Ahmes's real voice; ElevenLabs music at about 104 bpm that drops out on the sandbox stop.
- **Length:** 2:20–2:45.

## B. Inside Ahmes's head
- **Device and form:** the object is the explanation, an endless zoom.
- **What you see:** the camera dives into his antenna and follows one sentence through the machine: the Nemotron safety gate, the Qwen compiler, the gVisor box, the arithmetic grader, and Thoth watching. Each is a room. At the end it zooms back out into the real app.
- **First 2 s:** "Look at the tenths first." types out in a bubble, then the camera follows it into his head.
- **Beats:** hook → the safety gate → the compiler → the box (the dizzy rule is killed, with the real receipt) → the grader (24/40, then 40/40) → Thoth → zoom out to the real app → end card.
- **Look:** the same palette. Each room is built from the app's own parts.
- **Sound:** a narrator-led tour, a pulse that speeds up inside the box, and silence at the kill.
- **Strength:** the best fit for the "Application of Technology" score. The child and the story come second.
- **Length:** 2:00–2:30.

## C. Case file: who wrote the dangerous code? (the bold one)
- **Device and form:** the result first, split screen to one.
- **What you see:** the film opens on a real receipt: "Killed: used more than 64 MB." Then it rewinds. Who wrote this? An AI did, from a 10-year-old's words. It plays like a case file. The real receipts and recordings are the evidence, and the cartoon scenes re-enact what happened.
- **First 2 s:** the real receipt slams onto a desk, and a red "Killed" stamp lands.
- **Beats:** the evidence → the rewind → the child's sentence → the code it became → the box that caught it → the other exhibits (network, timeout, fork bomb) → the verdict: he learns, nothing breaks → end card.
- **Look:** graph paper as the desk, receipts as paper exhibits, one red stamp colour.
- **Sound:** a low tense pulse that resolves into the playful theme at the verdict.
- **Risk:** the most memorable for security judges, but the least like a children's app.
- **Length:** 1:45–2:15.

## Assumptions
- 16:9 at 1080p.
- Voices: the narrator, the child, Ahmes, and Thoth's voice from v2.
- ElevenLabs for the voices, sound effects and music (the Keychain key has music and sound-effects access).
- The real proof clips come from the v2 Playwright recordings (`video/out/work2/clips`), so no new screen recording is needed.
- Veo isn't needed. One generated establishing shot could be added later, but it would break the drawn style. Nemotron omni can't generate video.
- Paid items: none beyond the plans you already have.
