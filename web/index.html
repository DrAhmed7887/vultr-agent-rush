    Named after Ahmes, the scribe who copied the Rhind Papyrus around 1550 BC, a famous early maths text full of fractions. 3,500 years later, his robot descendant still gets them wrong.
    
    
    Every AI call runs on Vultr Serverless Inference (qwen3.8-27b). Ahmes's voice is ElevenLabs, and it only ever reads his own lines. What you type is held in memory for 24 hours at most and isn't saved to our database; please don't type personal details. A prototype; learning outcomes are untested.
