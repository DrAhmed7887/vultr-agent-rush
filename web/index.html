        Named after Ahmes, the scribe who copied the Rhind Papyrus around 1550 BC, a famous early maths text full of fractions. 3,500 years later, his robot descendant still gets them wrong.
        
    
        Every AI call runs on Vultr Serverless Inference: qwen3.8-27b writes the code, GLM-5.3 (Thoth, the teacher) checks it against the child's words, Nemotron omni hears the child, Nemotron safety checks the words. Ahmes's voice is ElevenLabs, and it only ever reads his own lines. What you say or type is held in memory for 24 hours at most and isn't saved; recordings aren't kept. Please don't share personal details. A prototype; learning outcomes are untested.
